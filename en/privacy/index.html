Privacy Policy — Cambios Vida

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy applies to Cambios Vida's customer service on WhatsApp through "Cambios Vida Conexión", the integration we use to connect our WhatsApp number to our customer service system. It explains which personal data we process in that service, for what purposes and on what legal basis, with whom we share it, how long we keep it and how you can exercise your rights, under Brazil's General Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018, "LGPD").

1. Who is responsible for your data

Controller: COMERCIOS VIDA RR LTDA, CNPJ 62.296.257/0001-90, Rua das Bromélias, s/n, bairro Orquídea, Pacaraima – RR, CEP 69345-000, Brazil.

Email: cambiosvidabr@gmail.com · WhatsApp: +55 95 99146-0413

Data protection officer ("encarregada", LGPD art. 41): Paola de los Angeles Pena Bolivar — cambiosvidabr@gmail.com. This is the channel for any matter about your data and for communications from Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

2. What data we process

  • Your WhatsApp contact: your number and your profile name.
  • What you send us: text messages, photos and PDF documents (such as payment receipts), the options you choose in buttons and what you fill in the form on our website, which you open with a button in WhatsApp. Audio, video, stickers, locations and contacts are not processed: the assistant replies that it cannot process them.
  • The data of each operation: amounts, rate, date, time and status. If you have approved credit with us, also the limit and the balance you owe.
  • The data of the person who receives the money: in Venezuela, the name, ID number (cédula), bank and account number, or the Pago Móvil phone number; in Brazil, the Pix key and the account holder's name. We ask for this data in a form on a page of ours, which you open with a button in WhatsApp, so that the account number and the ID number do not need to go through WhatsApp. It often belongs to another person: only give us data that person allowed you to share with us.
  • Payment data: the name and the CPF or CNPJ of the person who makes the Pix, the amount, the date and time and the transaction ID, which we read from the receipt and from our bank statement.
  • Our bank informs us of the name, CPF or CNPJ, amount and time of every Pix received in our account, including from people who are not our customers.
  • Today the assistant does not ask for your CPF. It reaches us through the receipt or the bank statement, and we link it to your record when a photo you sent shows it.
  • Your customer category (retail or VIP) and, if you are VIP, your weekly volume, which the system uses to set your category for the following week.
  • Technical records of the system and the server, which may contain your number, your profile name and the beginning of your messages.

3. What we use your data for, and the legal basis (LGPD art. 7)

  • To carry out the currency exchange you request: quote, prepare the summary, receive and check the payment and make the transfer. Basis: performance of a contract and of preliminary procedures, at your request (art. 7, V).
  • To confirm that each payment is yours and to prevent fraud, duplicate payments and transfers to the wrong person. Basis: performance of a contract (art. 7, V) and legitimate interest (art. 7, IX).
  • To calculate your rate and manage the credit of those who have it. Basis: performance of a contract (art. 7, V).
  • To comply with the legal and regulatory obligations of our activity, such as tax and accounting obligations, and to answer requests from authorities that have a legal basis. Basis: compliance with a legal or regulatory obligation (art. 7, II).
  • To exercise rights in judicial, administrative or arbitration proceedings. Basis: art. 7, VI.
  • To keep the system working, secure and free of errors. Basis: legitimate interest (art. 7, IX).

We do not sell your data or give it to anyone for advertising. We do not rely on your consent as the basis for processing your data.

4. Artificial intelligence and automated decisions

An artificial intelligence model (Claude, by Anthropic) reads your messages and the photos and PDFs you send, and drafts the replies. The amounts and rates of each operation are calculated by our system, not by the artificial intelligence. Some decisions are made by the system alone, for example whether a Pix in our bank statement belongs to your operation, and your VIP category each week. When the system cannot confirm a payment, a person on our team reviews it. You can ask for a person to review a decision made solely by automated means, and ask us for the criteria the system uses (art. 20).

5. Who we share your data with

We are the controller of your data. Anthropic, Supabase and Railway process it on our behalf (as processors), only for what is described here; Meta and the banks also process it under their own rules and policies.

  • Meta (WhatsApp): where the conversation takes place.
  • Anthropic, PBC, a United States company: the artificial intelligence assistant. It receives the messages of the day's conversation, the photos and PDFs you send and the data of the operation in progress, including the recipient's data. It stores the data in the United States and may also process it in countries in Europe and Asia and in Australia.
  • Supabase: stores the database and the images, on servers in São Paulo, Brazil.
  • Railway, in the United States: the server where our system runs. All data passes through it, and the technical records are kept there.
  • Cora, our bank in Brazil: informs us of the Pix received in our account.
  • The banks we use to make the transfers (in Brazil, the Pix of the reais; in Venezuela, the bank transfer or Pago Móvil of the bolívares): they receive the recipient data the transfer needs.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

6. Data processed outside Brazil (international transfer)

Part of the processing takes place outside Brazil:

  • in the United States: at Railway (the server) and at Anthropic (the assistant), which may also process the data in countries in Europe and Asia and in Australia;
  • on Meta (WhatsApp) servers, which may be in other countries;
  • by our providers' technical support, which may access the data from other countries;
  • in Venezuela: at the banks that receive the recipient data to make the bolívar transfers.

We make these transfers because they are necessary to provide the service you request, to comply with legal obligations and to exercise our rights (LGPD art. 33, IX, together with art. 7, V, II and VI; art. 9 of the Regulation approved by ANPD Resolution CD/ANPD No. 19/2024). We send only what each purpose needs, for the periods in section 7, with the security measures in section 8. To ask about these transfers or to exercise your rights, use the channel in section 9; you can also petition the ANPD.

7. How long we keep your data

  • The technical record of each message you send and of each message the assistant sends: 7 days.
  • The conversation the assistant uses: it is replaced when you write again on another day; if you do not come back, it is deleted in the monthly cleanup, usually one to two months later.
  • The system's operation records: up to 90 days.
  • The server's technical records: a few days, according to the provider's period.
  • The database backups: according to the provider's period.
  • The system's working data (internal queues and notes, and the destination accounts you asked us to discard): deleted automatically when no longer needed, usually between one hour and seven days.
  • Your customer record: until the end of the fifth year after the year of your last operation.
  • Everything else (the operations, the data of payers and recipients, the Pix received, the receipts and images and the messages received while our service is paused): until the end of the fifth year after the year of the operation or of the record (for example, those from 2026 until December 31, 2031). We keep them for this period to comply with legal obligations (art. 16, I) and to exercise our rights.

When the period ends, we delete the data or anonymize it.

8. Security

Only our system and the people on our team who maintain it access the database and the images. Connections between services are encrypted, and the access keys to our providers (WhatsApp and the bank) are stored encrypted. If a security incident may cause you relevant risk or harm, we will notify the ANPD and you, as the LGPD requires (art. 48).

9. Your rights and how to exercise them

Under LGPD art. 18, you can ask us, free of charge, for:

  • confirmation that we process your data, and access to it;
  • correction of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
  • anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary or excessive data, or of data processed in breach of the law;
  • portability of your data to another provider, under the ANPD's regulations;
  • deletion of your data (see below what must be kept);
  • information on who we share your data with;
  • information on the possibility of not giving consent and its consequences, and withdrawal of consent, if we ever ask for it (we do not use it today);
  • objection to processing that breaches the law;
  • review of a decision made solely by automated means (art. 20).

You can also petition Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

How to ask: write to cambiosvidabr@gmail.com telling us what you ask for and the WhatsApp number you used with us. To make sure the request is yours, we may ask you to confirm it on that WhatsApp. We reply within 15 days.

How to request the deletion of your data

  1. Send an email to cambiosvidabr@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data", telling us the WhatsApp number you used with us.
  2. We may ask you to confirm the request on that WhatsApp.
  3. Within 15 days, we tell you what we deleted and what must be kept, and why.

What may need to be kept: the data of an operation in progress, until it ends; the data of a pending balance, until it is paid; and the data the law requires us to keep, until the end of its period in section 7.

The messages on your phone and on WhatsApp's servers do not depend on us: to delete them, use WhatsApp's own options.

10. Minors

Our service is only for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly process data of minors; if we learn that a minor wrote to us, we do not carry out the operation and we delete their data, except the data the law requires us to keep.

11. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes too. If the Portuguese, Spanish and English versions differ, the Portuguese version prevails.
