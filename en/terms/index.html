Last updated: November 24, 2026

These terms apply to Cambios Vida's customer service on WhatsApp through "Cambios Vida Conexión", the integration we use to connect our WhatsApp number to our customer service system.

2. The service

Exchange of Brazilian reais (R$) into Venezuelan bolívares (Bs), and of bolívares into reais, contracted on WhatsApp. An automated assistant with artificial intelligence serves you, and our team follows each operation and makes the transfers. The service is only for people aged 18 or over.

3. How an operation works

  • a) The assistant gives you the rate. You give us the recipient's data in a form on a page of ours, which you open with a button in WhatsApp (so the account number and the ID number do not go through WhatsApp). With it, the assistant prepares a summary with the amounts and the recipient's data, showing the account number and the ID number only by their last digits. Check everything before you confirm: the transfer goes to the data you confirmed.
  • b) From reais to bolívares: you make the Pix to the key of the account in the name of COMERCIOS VIDA RR LTDA that we give you in the WhatsApp chat, and you send us the receipt. The operation is registered when we receive a receipt with the data that lets us find your payment (name, CPF, date and time, amount and transaction ID); if data is missing, we ask you for the complete receipt.
  • c) From bolívares to reais: you pay the bolívares to the account in Venezuela that we show you on a page of ours, which you open with a button in WhatsApp. That account is not in the company's name but in a person's name; the page shows you that person's name and ID. Then we send you the reais by Pix, to the key you gave us in the form.
  • d) A payment counts as received only when our system finds it in our bank statement or when our team confirms it.
  • e) While our service is open and our team is available, the transfer is usually sent 5 to 15 minutes after the payment is confirmed. Otherwise, it is sent when we are back.

4. Rate and amounts

The rate depends on your customer category and on the amount. The rate and amounts of your operation appear in the summary you confirm on WhatsApp.

  • From reais to bolívares, the rate in force at the time of your payment applies, according to our bank. If it is lower than the last rate the assistant showed you that same day, before going on it asks whether you accept the new rate or prefer a refund.
  • From bolívares to reais, and in a credit operation, the rate at the time the operation is registered applies.

Once your operation is registered, a change in the day's rate does not change it.

5. Fees

We do not charge commissions or separate fees: the price is in the rate. Your bank may charge its own fees.

6. Limits

The minimum amount per operation is R$ 10. We may limit or pause new operations (for example, when we do not have bolívares available) and refuse an operation.

7. Withdrawal

You can withdraw through the same WhatsApp or by email. In an operation from reais to bolívares, if you withdraw after paying and before the transfer, we ask whether you prefer a refund or the transfer to another account. Note: once the transfer has been sent, it cannot be reversed.

8. Hours

The assistant serves every day from 08:00 to 23:00, Roraima and Venezuela time (UTC−4). Outside these hours it only registers payments already made, and the operation continues when we are back. We may pause our service; the assistant tells you.

9. Prohibited use

We do not accept operations for unlawful purposes, and we may refuse an operation.

10. Customer service and complaints

For complaints, write to cambiosvidabr@gmail.com: we confirm that we received it and reply within 5 days. For anything else, our WhatsApp.

11. Privacy

Your data is processed as described in our Privacy Policy (https://cambiosvida.com.br/en/privacy/).

12. Law and jurisdiction

Brazilian law applies, including the Consumer Protection Code. You may file a lawsuit in the courts of your domicile, if you live in Brazil, or in the judicial district (comarca) of Pacaraima, Roraima, where we are based.
